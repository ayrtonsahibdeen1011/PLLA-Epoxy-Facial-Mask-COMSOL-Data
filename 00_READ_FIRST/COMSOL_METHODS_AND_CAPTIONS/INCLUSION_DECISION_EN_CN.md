# Decision on the corrected open corner model
# 正确开角模型的纳入结论
**Include the corrected model as supplementary configuration screening. Do not use it as evidence that removing corners improves impact protection or ventilation.**
**建议作为补充材料纳入，用于整体几何方案的数值对照。不要据此宣称去角提高冲击防护或通风性能。**
The repaired geometry preserves the intended openings. At nominal 150 mm width, the integrated solid volume is 86.920 cm3 for O and 43.271 cm3 for A. Calculated mass under the assumed density is 121.69 and 60.579 g. These are model-derived quantities, not weighed samples. The designs differ in material amount, thickness distribution, curvature and coverage.
修复保留了设计开口。两者在相同名义宽度下的体积与厚度分布不同，因此比较的是完整方案，而不是严格控制其他变量后的单独去角效应。计算质量不是试样称重。
O cools more slowly in the prescribed free-cooling scenario: its volume-mean temperature at 300 s is 306.2150 K, compared with 300.6314 K in A. The difference is 5.5836 K. The convection coefficient is imposed, with no solved airflow. The calculation therefore does not quantify actual ventilation.
在给定冷却工况下，O 在300秒时的平均温度更高。模型施加对流系数，没有求解流场，故不能把这个结果写成实测通风优劣。
With identical material assumptions, a normalized 2.70025832 N Gaussian load and coordinate-defined lateral support regions, O is less compliant: maximum displacement is 0.0017083 mm versus 0.015158 mm for A. Mean von Mises stress is 0.0087715 versus 0.034980 MPa. These are small-load stationary responses, not football-impact predictions or failure thresholds.
在共同材料假设、同等合力和坐标定义的侧边支承下，O 的位移及平均应力较低。这属于小载荷静力筛选，不是足球动态冲击或失效预测。
The useful conclusion is a conditional stiffness–cooling tradeoff associated with the complete configurations. The larger material volume is a major confounder and a plausible contributor; its isolated contribution was not computed. Neither the sign nor magnitude can be attributed solely to open corners.
可采用的结论是：在所给假设下，两种整体方案体现了刚度与冷却之间的权衡。较大的材料体积是重要混杂因素，不能把全部差异归因于去角。

Use one short numerical paragraph in the main text that points to S01–S04 and Tables T01–T02. Keep the full geometry views, verification tables and optional field plots in supplementary material. Do not revive the earlier flat-B comparison as the open-corner comparison. The older flat study remains a separately identified archive.
