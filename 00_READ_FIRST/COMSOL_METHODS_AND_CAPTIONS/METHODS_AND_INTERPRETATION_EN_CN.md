# Numerical methods and interpretation
# 数值方法与结果解释
## Study scope
Three distinct model identities must be kept separate. A is the existing curved mask geometry, translated and normalized to 150 mm width. O is the user-identified open-corner STL, repaired in a derived copy, aligned to a common coordinate frame and normalized to nominal 150 mm width. H is the repaired historical sphere-contact family at its original scale. The previous flat 3MF configuration is not O.
A 为现有曲面几何；O 为本次确认并修复的开角 STL；H 为原始尺度的历史球面接触模型。旧的平面3MF并非O。三组模型不可混用名称、尺度或实验对应关系。
## Geometry and material representation
The original O STL contains near-coincident duplicate seams and intersecting facets. The seam-welded copy retained all 3972 triangles. Subsequent voxel remeshing at 0.2 mm, five local smoothing iterations and decimation produced a closed 36000-triangle derived surface. No intentional opening was capped. A second repair at 0.25 mm with eight smoothing iterations and 18000 triangles tested the effect of repair choices. These are documented geometric approximations. The primary repair had a maximum sampled source-surface deviation of 0.1604 mm and a 99th-percentile deviation of 0.0547 mm. The source-to-repair sampled distance is not a rigorous Hausdorff bound. Source algebraic volume changed by -0.0330%; that source volume is only a diagnostic because the original surface intersects. Finite-element integrated volumes are reported separately from straight-sided STL/tetrahedral volumes.
O 原文件含极小重复接缝及相交三角面。修复过程保留设计开口，并记录每一步的尺度、体积和表面偏差。主修复版本的最大抽样表面偏差为0.1604 mm，99百分位偏差为0.0547 mm；这不是严格的Hausdorff距离上界。原表面存在相交，因此其代数体积仅用于诊断。有限元积分体积与STL线性面体积分别列出。
Both configurations use the same homogeneous isotropic material. Density is 1400 kg/m3, Young modulus 3.5 GPa and Poisson ratio 0.38. Thermal conductivity is 0.25 W/(m K) and heat capacity is 1000 J/(kg K). These effective inputs are assumptions inherited for controlled numerical screening. They are not fitted to new coupon or impact data. PLLA fibres, epoxy layers, interfaces, fibre alignment, damage, plasticity, fracture and viscoelastic energy dissipation are not resolved. Consequently these calculations cannot rank resin products or validate reinforcement mechanisms.
两方案采用相同均匀各向同性材料参数。这些参数用于受控数值筛选，不是最新试样拟合值。未显式模拟纤维、分层、界面、取向、断裂或黏弹耗能，不能据此比较树脂品牌或验证增强机理。
## Free cooling
The heat equation is rho Cp dT/dt = div(k grad T), with initial temperature 318.15 K. All exterior boundaries use outward conductive heat flux h(T-Ta), where Ta = 293.15 K and h = 8 W/(m2 K). Separate h = 4 and 16 cases provide a factor-of-two boundary sensitivity. No heat generation or radiation is included. Both sides of the mask exchange heat with the same environment. There is no fluid-flow solution.
采用固体热传导及外表面对流边界。初温318.15 K，环境293.15 K，基准h为8 W/(m2 K)，并计算4和16。未加入内热源、辐射或流场。
O was solved with adaptive BDF time integration, maximum order five, direct linear solves and relative tolerance 1e-6. Saved output spacing is 5 s from 0 to 900 s. A tolerance check uses 1e-5. Spatial and independent repair checks use 0, 60, 300 and 900 s. A retains its earlier verified solutions: dense curves at 1e-5 and four 1e-6 verification times. Accordingly the A dense-curve value at 300 s differs from its verification value by about 0.0052 K. Figure metadata identifies the exact series rather than silently mixing them.
O 的基准时间容差为1e-6，最大BDF阶数为5，另用1e-5核验。A沿用已核验结果，密集曲线与较严格检查点的微小差异在图表说明中明确列出。
The energy check compares rho Cp integral(T-Ta)dV with the trapezoidal time integral of exterior heat loss. A separate rate check compares integral(rho Cp dT/dt)dV with outward heat loss. Trapezoidal quadrature has finite error. Bulk mean temperatures are the accepted response; tiny initial local interpolation excursions are not physical heating.
能量验证同时核查总热量变化、对流散热时间积分及瞬时热量变化率。主要报告体积平均温度；初始局部微小数值过冲不是物理升温。
## Stationary mechanical screening
Each aligned model receives the same spatial Gaussian weight g = I(nz > 0.2) exp[-(x/18 mm)^2-(y/18 mm)^2] on the exterior surface. The applied traction is directed along negative z and normalized by the surface integral of g to give exactly 2.70025832 N. Different surface coverage makes the integrated load centroid slightly different; the recorded centroids must not be described as identical experimental contact patches.
外表面采用同一坐标定义的高斯分布，并通过面积积分归一化，使两者合力一致。几何覆盖不同，载荷积分中心略有差异，不应声称接触斑完全相同。
To avoid dependence on imported face IDs, supports are applied as restoring traction -K gfix (u,v,w), where gfix = 0.5[1+tanh((abs(x)-xs)/0.25 mm)]. The baseline xs is 65 mm and K is 1e12 N/m3. Checks use xs = 60 mm and K = 1e13 N/m3. This is an idealized high-stiffness distributed support, not a measured fixture. It is not an exactly prescribed zero-displacement boundary. The prior centroid-of-face fixed selection was rejected because changing surface segmentation altered the effective clamp area.
为避免导入面的分组影响支承范围，采用坐标定义的高刚度分布弹性支承。基准阈值65 mm、刚度1e12 N/m3，另核验60 mm和1e13。它是理想化近似夹持，不是实测夹具，也不是严格零位移约束。旧的按整面中心选固定面的结果已排除。
The model solves small-strain stationary elasticity with quadratic displacement interpolation. Reported responses are volume-mean von Mises stress, maximum displacement and total elastic strain energy in the mask. Force balance includes the integrated restoring traction. Work verification uses applied traction dotted with displacement, equal to twice the sum of mask and support elastic energies. These checks do not establish contact dynamics or absorbed impact energy. Local stress maxima near supports are excluded from strength conclusions.
静力验证包含合力平衡以及外载做功与面罩、弹性支承储能的对应关系。弹性储能不等于冲击耗散能；局部支承峰值不用于失效判断。
## Historical contact family
The historical study uses a 210 mm sphere and the original mask scale, rather than the 150 mm A/O comparison. Only the verified loaded subset at 1.5–4 mm prescribed movement is released. Central and upper-orbital loading are compared at equal imposed movement, which produces unequal contact force. The regional radius is 150 mm in this historical coordinate system and the intersected material volumes differ. At 4 mm, the reported contact forces are approximately 953.5 N centrally and 416.0 N orbitally; regional mean stresses are approximately 4.823 and 3.208 MPa. These are not calibrated sensor forces or injury thresholds. The unverified onset interval and old union-contact models are excluded.
历史接触模型保持原始尺度，球直径210 mm；仅使用已验证的1.5至4 mm位移段。相同位移下接触力不同，区域取样体积也不同。不得把该组数据与150 mm方案直接混为同一试样或同一实验标定。
